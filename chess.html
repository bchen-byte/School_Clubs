Chess Club Information

Welcome to the Belize High School Chess Club! We are here to guide you if you are a beginner, and if you are a seasoned player, we can give you tournaments to play in. We are looking to create a friendly environment where everyone can help each other and expand on their chess skills. We meet weekly in the chemistry lab. Boards and peices are provided so, just join us and play! We also have online and offline tournaments monthly, where we compete against other chess clubs. organizations, and communities.

Our purpose:

We want to guide everyone in this club and bring people togther via chess.
